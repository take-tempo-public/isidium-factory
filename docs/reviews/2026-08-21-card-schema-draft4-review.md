## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**The core is dry.** Three independent clean-room hashers agree byte-for-byte on build and claims hashes across the whole corpus; the single-entry chain and `sign(tenant ‖ h)` verify, and the collapsed signature catches the consistent-rewrite fixture the draft-3 tuple could not; every oracle disagreement is a ruled change, none an underspecification. **The periphery is not** — and the two lenses the owner added found it from opposite directions. The bloat refuter's finding is systematic: almost every fact is stored twice (the attestation on every signed entry, the fingerprint's copies of its own entry, the sidecar's closure mirror, `status` and `hold` stored though derivable, a full history mirror in the ledger, a journal row that copies the entry it journals); several properties the one `write` function already prevents are then detected by two or three more labels; one function is fronted by **28 verbs and 6 roles**; one fact is named in **three vocabularies**; and the draft-key/promote machinery, per-commit board regeneration, two merge drivers, and chained read-journaling each add a write, a file, an install step or an O(T) pass to the common path. Its call-count table puts a card's lifetime at 5–6 store calls and ~16 hashes as written against 3 calls and ~7 hashes in the leaner form — with nothing lost the owner has named. The implementer confirms the same periphery is where the last unbuildable points sit: the batch position is inside the chain hash it is derived from (circular); re-ratification has no derivable act; the hook cannot recompute a commit carrying two entries; a first-match act table lets a contributor clear a blocked hold by re-kinding it; `merge-history` re-sequences signed entries and voids the owner's signature; promotion has three chain readings. The Rust refuter's verdict is the same sentence from a third side: the hashing core is within a dozen one-line pins of mechanically portable; what would not port is seven open enums, two unparseable byte formats (`att`'s envelope, the WebAuthn concatenation), a journal row that cannot describe multi-file writes, floats and an unpinned regex dialect inside hashed content, and an unstated two-model architecture. Nearly all of round 5 is **subtraction**. Ten calls are the owner's (section 1) because they reopen things ratified before the efficiency rule existed, and the owner should re-decide them with the price visible; the rest is a pinning pass (section 2).

## 2. Fill worklist for draft-5 (mechanics; no ruling needed)

## 4. What the refuters verified and could not break

Three hashers agree on build and claims bytes across CRLF, key order, NFC, comments, tending edits and footer appends; the footer append preserves CRLF and re-hashes; CAS refuses a stale base; single-signature bytes are exactly `tenant ‖ "\n" ‖ h` and a wrong tenant fails; every act is reachable through a verb, appends one entry, and its act/fields/build recompute from the (before, after) the write saw, with every role refusal the matrix requires (38 steps, 0 flagged); the diff-based guard (W3) closes the masking; reconciliation explains create, a two-write commit, a store revert, a two-path write, and accepts `repair --journal` while flagging the `--no-verify` edit.
